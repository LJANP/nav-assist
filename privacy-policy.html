Privacy Policy — NavAssist

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Data Collection

NavAssist does not transmit or share any data. Everything the extension processes stays entirely within your browser on your own device. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Data Storage

The extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage APIs:

  • Export field preferences (which columns to include when exporting)
  • Message templates (subject and message text you write)
  • Temporarily captured prospect data during an active export session
  • WhatsApp message templates you write
  • Temporarily recorded WhatsApp touchpoints during an active session — the phone number or name shown in the conversation header, the message text you pasted, and the date

Session data is cleared whenever you use the extension's Reset button. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

External Communication

NavAssist makes zero network requests. No data is sent to any external server, API, analytics service, or third party.

Clipboard

When you click an export or "Send to SFDC" button, NavAssist copies the relevant data to your system clipboard so you can paste it into another tool of your choosing. This is an explicit action you take; the extension never writes to the clipboard on its own, and it does not read your clipboard.

Permissions

  • Storage: Used to save your preferences and templates locally.
  • Host permission (linkedin.com): Required for the extension to read prospect information from LinkedIn pages and display its UI elements.
  • Host permission (web.whatsapp.com): Required for the extension to display its UI elements in WhatsApp Web and to read the open conversation's header so the interaction can be logged against the right contact.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
